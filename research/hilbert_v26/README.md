## Exact construction and obstruction
Let F(t)=exp(-t/2)(psi(exp(t))-exp(t)) for t>=0. Extend the windowed signal F_T by zero outside [0,T]. On L2(R,dt), U_h f(t)=f(t+h) is a strongly continuous unitary group by translation invariance of Lebesgue measure. The closed span of all translates of F_T is an invariant Hilbert subspace; its inner product is inherited from L2 and therefore positive. This construction is unconditional for every finite T and applies to any square-integrable windowed signal. It does not distinguish the arithmetic signal from a growing control.

Under Fourier transform the generator -i d/dt is multiplication by real frequency on its natural domain. It has no L2 eigenfunctions: an eigenfunction's Fourier transform must be supported on a singleton, a Lebesgue-null set. Restricting to a closed translation-invariant subspace does not supply discrete eigenvalues either. Peaks in a finite FFT are not eigenvalues of this generator. Periodic finite-dimensional cyclic shifts have discrete grid frequencies selected by the window, not by zeta zeros. A positive Hilbert space and real spectrum therefore do not establish spectral completeness for RH.

For the artificial signal g_T(t)=exp((delta+i omega)t) 1_[0,T](t), normalized autocorrelation at 0<=h<T equals exp(i omega h) exp(-delta h) [1-exp(-2 delta(T-h))]/[1-exp(-2 delta T)] for delta>0; at delta=0 it is exp(i omega h)(1-h/T). For positive delta its pointwise limit is exp(i omega h-delta |h|) (extend to negative h by conjugation). This is positive definite: its Fourier measure is a positive Cauchy density centered at omega. Thus the normalized limiting correlation of a truly growing mode can still have a positive spectral representation. The normalization discards the absolute growth scale. This formalizes the light/shadow, infinite spiral, compression, and six-eared macaque metaphors. The proof holds independent of numerical tests.

## Remaining proof obligation
Let F_e(t)=exp(-e t)F(t) for t>=0, zero elsewhere. Its L2 norm squared is exactly integral_1^infinity |psi(x)-x|^2 x^(-2-2e) dx. For every e>0, finiteness of this integral is equivalent to RH: RH implies the prime discrepancy bound with any smaller exponent; conversely Cauchy-Schwarz makes the Mellin integral of E(x)=psi(x)-x holomorphic for Re(s)>1/2+e. The exact identity (log((s-1)zeta(s)))'=1-s integral_1^infinity E(x)x^(-s-1)dx, initially Re(s)>1, then excludes poles at zeros there; reflection gives confinement. Reference: https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.16. This is the v1.3 weighted-energy condition in Hilbert-space language, not a new independent estimate. A single damping e can hide growth delta<e, so every e is necessary. No unconditional near-critical weighted bound or complete discrete spectral correspondence is established.
