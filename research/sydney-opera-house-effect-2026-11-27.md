## Working interpretation
Scientific inquiry is constrained by the tools, representations, measurement boundaries and methods through which a phenomenon becomes observable and testable. A successful model may accurately describe aspects of reality while omitting information outside its scope. An elegant construction is not proof that the underlying system has the same structure.

This interpretation connects to the BCE principle “Every description is a model, not the thing itself.” It suggests testing whether changing a representation changes a conclusion, and separating measurement constraints from intrinsic constraints. It does not imply that science is invalid or that any particular untested alternative is true.
