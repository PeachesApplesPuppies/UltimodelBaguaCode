# BCE v1.1 — Multi-side visibility audit
Run `python -m pip install mpmath sympy`, then `python engine.py`. Ctrl+C exits; results are atomically replaced on completion. Archived v1.0 arithmetic data is an input, not overwritten.

Independent numerical extraction of Li coefficients via Cauchy sampling of log(xi(1/(1-z))/xi(1)) on |z|=1/2, at 96 and 128 nodes, 75 decimal digits. Principal logarithm samples are used; numerical agreement is not an interval certificate.

Exact adversarial multiplier: y=s(s-1), y0=rho(rho-1), rho=3/4+20i, Q(y)=y^41(y+1/4)^8, c=Q(y0). P(s)=((Q(y)-Re c)^2+(Im c)^2)/|c|^2. P is reflection- and conjugation-symmetric, positive for real y, vanishes at rho, equals 1+O((s-1)^41) and 1+O((s-1/2)^16). Thus xi*P preserves the first 40 Li coefficients and centered derivatives through order 15, while having an injected off-line zero. It also preserves order one, but is NOT xi and need not satisfy the Euler product or the zeta zero strip restriction for all added roots. This is a logical control, not evidence of a real zeta counterexample.

Toy model: four off-line zeros at 1/4 and 3/4, heights +/-20, and two on-line zeros at height +/-14. Li sums by direct powers are numerical, not validated. The transformed pair at +20 has squared separation -1/4, so the Hermite test rejects it exactly. The generating function logarithmic derivative has a pole inside the disk at z=1-1/rho, with residue equal to multiplicity. No other distinct zero maps to that pole. Hence cancellation of finitely many coefficients cannot remove the pole.

Remaining analytic obligation: bound the Li coefficient exponential growth unconditionally, e.g. limsup |lambda_n|^(1/n)<=1, equivalent to analyticity of the generating logarithmic derivative in the unit disk and hence RH. No RH proof is claimed.
