# BCE v1.2 — Arithmetic and Singularity Engine

Run `python -m pip install -r requirements.txt` and `python engine.py`. Ctrl+C interrupts; each completed stage is atomically written to results.json. Rerunning recomputes stages. Archived inputs are unchanged. The tests use numerical arithmetic (not validated interval arithmetic), except the rational reciprocal checks. No proof of RH is claimed.

Let Z(s)=(s-1)zeta(s), s=1/(1-z). The arithmetic generating function B(z)=sum B_n z^(n-1)=Z'(s)/[Z(s)(1-z)^2]. For Re(s)>1, Z'/Z=1/(s-1)-sum Lambda(k)/k^s. Integration by parts gives Z'/Z=1-s*integral_1^infinity (psi(x)-x)x^(-s-1)dx. If psi(x)-x=O_epsilon(x^(1/2+epsilon)) for every epsilon>0, the integral continues analytically to Re(s)>1/2. That bound is itself equivalent to RH, not assumed here. A pole at a nontrivial zero rho has residue equal to its multiplicity in B(z), at z=1-1/rho. Reflection sends this point to its reciprocal conjugate. No cancellation of distinct poles is possible. The Cauchy-Hadamard formula then makes limsup |B_n|^(1/n)<=1 equivalent to RH.

The analytic Li contribution A_n is 1-n*(gamma+log(4*pi))/2 plus the sum over positive odd j of [(1-1/j)^n-1+n/j]. Each bracket is nonnegative, bounded by n/j and n(n-1)/(2*j^2). Split at j=n and use the harmonic and integral bounds to get |A_n|<=1+n*abs(gamma+log(4*pi))/2+n*(1+log(n))+(n-1)/2. Thus analytic contributions cannot generate or cancel exponential coefficient growth in the limsup sense. The odd-denominator sum is independently evaluated using its first 100 terms and a Hurwitz-zeta tail; comparison is against rounded archived numbers, not a new certified result.

Synthetic quartet at real parts 1/4 and 3/4 and heights +/-T, with an on-line pair at +/-14, has interior squared pole radius (16*T*T+1)/(16*T*T+9). The coefficient exponential rate is (1/2)*log((16*T*T+9)/(16*T*T+1)), asymptotic to 1/(4*T*T). Finite block maxima are diagnostics, not certified rates. No artificial roots are claimed to be actual zeta zeros.

Prime-power check: for real s=2,3,4, use Lambda(k)<=log(k) and monotonicity of log(x)/x^s beyond N=100000. The omitted tail is at most N^(1-s)*(log(N)/(s-1)+1/(s-1)^2). Arithmetic rounding is not enclosed. This bound does not extend the Euler product into the critical strip.
