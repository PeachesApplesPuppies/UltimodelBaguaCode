# BCE v2.1 Distinct Prime Interaction Engine
Run `python -m pip install -r requirements.txt` and `python engine.py`. Ctrl+C exits with status 130. Results are atomically replaced on completion; an interrupted run leaves earlier results intact, and a rerun recomputes. No inputs are modified.

## Exact identities and scope
Let a(n)=1_prime(n) log(n)-1 for 1<=n<=N, zero otherwise. For integer H>=1, let C(d)=sum_n a(n)a(n+d). Then sum_m (sum_{j=1}^H a(m+j))^2 = H C(0)+2 sum_{d=1}^{H-1}(H-d)C(d), with all integer m and zero padding. This follows by counting ordered pairs of positions at each difference. For H=N, the left includes every prefix discrepancy square. Conversely it consists of prefix and suffix sums; suffix squares are bounded by twice the total discrepancy squared plus twice prefix squares. No claim of a uniform bound is made. FFT and direct computations use floating point; the identity is exact but these runs are not interval certificates.

For F(x)=theta(x)-x, D(x)=F(2x)-F(x), a sufficient and necessary RH condition is integral_1^X |D(x)|^2 dx = O_epsilon(X^(2+2epsilon)) for every epsilon>0. RH implies this by the classical prime-counting error bound (higher prime powers are O(sqrt(x)log x)). Conversely F(x)=sum_{k>=1}D(x/2^k) by telescoping since F(y)=-y for 0<y<1. Minkowski gives ||D(x/2^k)||_{L2(0,X)}=2^(k/2)||D||_{L2(0,X/2^k)}; using the assumed bound for X/2^k>=1 gives a summable geometric factor 2^(-k(1/2+epsilon)); the remaining tail is O(sqrt(X)) in norm. Thus integral_1^X |F|^2=O_epsilon(X^(2+2epsilon)). Dyadic summation with a smaller epsilon yields the finite weighted energy criterion of v1.3 and RH by Mellin holomorphy. The required D bound is not proved. Discrete samples in this engine are not continuous integral certificates.

## Modular obstruction
For wheel q=210, count residues r with gcd(r,q)=gcd(r+h,q)=1. Genuine prime pairs have exceptions involving 2,3,5,7. The artificial control assigns weights 35/4 to coprime integers for M periods, then zero for M periods. Total mass equals endpoint; every eligible residue has equal total mass. Midpoint discrepancy equals midpoint, not square root. Exact integer arithmetic checks the control using weights multiplied by four. This does not model the actual primes; it refutes sufficiency of wheel support plus endpoint and aggregate residue balance alone.
