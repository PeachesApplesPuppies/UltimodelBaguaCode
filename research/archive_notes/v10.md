# BCE v1.0 — arithmetic positivity audit

Run `python -m pip install mpmath`, then `python audit.py` in this directory. Ctrl+C interrupts. Results are replaced atomically after the computation. No zero locations are used. The two precision runs (65 and 90 decimal digits) are numerical cross-checks, not rigorous interval bounds. All first 40 Li coefficients are positive. No general positivity proof is claimed.

The generating function is log xi(1/(1-z)) = log xi(1) + sum lambda_n z^n/n. With Z(s)=(s-1)zeta(s), expand Z'/Z = sum b_k (s-1)^k. The recursion is b_k=(k+1)c_(k+1)-sum_(j=0)^(k-1) b_j c_(k-j), where Z(1+x)=sum c_k x^k, c_0=1 and c_(k+1)=(-1)^k gamma_k/k!. Then B_n=sum binom(n,k)b_(k-1), A_n=1-n*(gamma+log(4*pi))/2+sum_(k=2)^n binom(n,k)(-1)^k(1-2^-k)zeta(k). Their sum is lambda_n.

Obstruction: reciprocal pairing alone permits u=2, 1/u=1/2, yielding 2-u-1/u=-1/2. Prime-power weights L_1^(1)(log m)=2-log m change sign between m=2 and m=11; termwise positivity does not establish the regularized prime expression. The exact outstanding obligation is B_n >= -A_n for all n, not just n<=40. Earlier finite-region Hermite tests and reciprocal invariants do not imply this bound.
