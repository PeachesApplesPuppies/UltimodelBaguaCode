# BCE v1.5 — Arithmetic recurrence proof audit
Run `python -m pip install numpy` and `python engine.py` from this directory. Ctrl+C interrupts; results are atomically replaced on successful completion. Inputs are not modified. Results use floating point for logarithms; the integer Mobius convolution check is exact.

For x a positive integer, psi(x)=sum_{d<=x} mu(d) log(floor(x/d)!). Also let B(x)=log(x!)-2log(floor(x/2)!). By the divisor identity, B(x)=sum_{k<=x}(-1)^(k+1) psi(x/k), with psi evaluated at the floor. Stirling and the alternating harmonic series give B(x)-x sum_{k<=x}(-1)^(k+1)/k=O(log(2x)). Thus E(x)=-sum_{2<=k<=x}(-1)^(k+1)E(x/k)+O(log(2x)), E=psi-x. These are unconditional. A naive square-root-weighted absolute sum of the recurrence coefficients diverges as x grows. Alternating cancellation cannot be inferred from this identity alone: for Re(s)>0, the infinite alternating dilation operator applied to x^s has multiplier eta(s)=(1-2^(1-s))zeta(s), and its truncation at k=floor(x) incurs O_s(1), by summation by parts. Consequently a hypothetical off-line zero gives an oscillatory mode satisfying the recurrence up to a bounded error. This does not establish such a zero exists; it identifies why the recurrence alone cannot exclude it. An independent arithmetic bound is still necessary. No RH proof is claimed.
