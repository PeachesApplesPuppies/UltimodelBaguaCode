## Analytic audit
For an invertible bounded operator K on a Hilbert space, if sup over all integer n of ||K^n|| <= C, then C^-2 I <= M_N <= C^2 I for M_N=(sum_{n=-N}^N (K^n)*K^n)/(2N+1). The weak operator compactness of a bounded ball gives a convergent subnet; endpoint telescoping implies invariance of the limit. Conversely a bounded coercive invariant metric implies uniform two-sided boundedness. This extends the v2.4 finite-dimensional theorem. It does not construct a prime-derived K or establish complete spectral correspondence. The shift on all of L2(R) is unitary, but placing the normalized prime discrepancy in an appropriate orbit space with a controlled norm is an additional unproved requirement; the usual weighted forward energy bound is itself equivalent to RH. No proof of RH claimed.
