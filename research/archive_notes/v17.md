For q>=2, a_q(k)=1-q*1_{q|k}, D_q(s)=(1-q^(1-s))*zeta(s). At a nontrivial zero rho of multiplicity m, D_q^(j)(rho)=0 for j<m and D_q^(m)(rho) != 0, since the base factor is nonzero in 0<Re(s)<1. The differentiated Dirichlet series converges locally uniformly for Re(s)>0 by bounded partial sums and summation by parts. For first derivative, the tail after N is bounded by (q-1)N^-sigma [log N+1/sigma+|s|(log N/sigma+1/sigma^2)].

Let E(x)=psi(x)-x for x>=1, zero below 1. Define T_j(x)=sum_{k<=x} a_q(k)(-log k)^j E(x/k). For Re(s)>1 absolute convergence permits interchange, and Mellin(T_j)=D_q^(j)(s) (1-Z'/Z)/s, Z=(s-1)zeta(s). At a nontrivial zero of order m, the j=m product has residue -m D_q^(m)(rho)/rho, nonzero; for j<m it is regular. Thus if for every j>=1 and epsilon>0 the integral of |T_j(x)|^2 x^(-2-2epsilon) from 1 to infinity is finite, Cauchy-Schwarz and analytic continuation exclude every zero right of 1/2; reflection gives RH.
