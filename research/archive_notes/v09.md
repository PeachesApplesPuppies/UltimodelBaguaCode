# BCE Moment Engine v0.9

Exact Hermite–Sylvester real-rootedness tests using Newton power sums and rational Schur complements. For a finite reflection-invariant multiset of zeta zeros, transform w=-i(s-1/2); the multiset is closed under conjugation. Its n-by-n Hermite matrix H_ij=m_(i+j), indexed from zero, is positive semidefinite iff every transformed zero is real. For any real polynomial q with coefficient vector v, v^T H v=sum q(w)^2. If a nonreal pair exists, real-coefficient interpolation assigns i and -i to the pair and zero to every other distinct root, yielding a strictly negative sum including multiplicities. Rank counts distinct roots by the Vandermonde factorization over the distinct roots (full column rank and nonzero multiplicities). For n=2, the determinant is 2m2-m1^2; this is minus the discriminant in the original s coordinates.

Run `python -m pip install -r requirements.txt` then `python engine.py` from this directory. Ctrl+C interrupts; completed stages are atomically saved to results.json. Rerunning recomputes the stages rather than resuming. Inputs are not modified. The archive contains the archived v0.8 results, not its executable.

129 exact rational control tests passed, including tiny imaginary pairs of magnitude 10^-40. The previous v0.8 summary was read and checked, not independently rerun. Exploratory zeta moments on [0,1] x [20,26] use composite midpoint contour integration of zeta'/zeta; no known zero locations supplied. These quadratures have no validated error bounds and are not certificates. No RH proof is claimed. Exact positivity for every symmetric zero-free-boundary region would be equivalent to RH, not an independent proof of it. Repeated roots may produce singular PSD matrices; interval uncertainty around zero must remain inconclusive. 

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01745 (Hermite–Sylvester criterion).
