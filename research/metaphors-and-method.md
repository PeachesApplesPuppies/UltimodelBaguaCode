## Earlier vocabulary and connections

Herzog's Phenomenal Stone: transform, invert, recover the hidden component, compare, and try to break the result. Obsidian Stone / Stone Ocean name bridges and perspectives, not physical materials with extraordinary powers. The scale-polarity example is exactly reversible when both numbers are retained; the anti-proof intentionally discards scale.

BaguaCode: a three-bit model of eight states, followed by an experimental virtual machine. The eight corners of a cube make changes observable by Hamming distance. Common signed-coordinate permutations preserve that distance. For three bits the family has 3! times 2^3 = 48 members. Arbitrary permutations need not preserve distance; a mapping can even collapse every input to one state. The Lens selects a representation or observer; Flow' may measure change per declared step; neither supplies a physical field equation by itself.

Infinite local spiral / Infinite Pie: recurring direction with changing magnitude; subdivision without a final finite iteration. Equal angle or phase is not equality of complete state. The Centerpoint is a chosen reference or an invariant of a specified transformation, not a universal consequence of recursion. A simple contraction x->x/2 need not respect a proposed identification of 0 with 1.

Light and shadow / polarity and inversion: paired descriptions may reveal hidden degrees of freedom, but they may also be noninvertible projections. Carry the missing magnitude, sign, index or integration constant explicitly.

Wormhole / address book / Equation Provenance Map: retain source, parent, transformation, version and counterexample so derivations can be navigated and replayed. A finite generator can produce an unbounded number of records over time, but a finite store cannot losslessly represent every arbitrary larger input without external information. Compression and emulation experiments are separate engineering work, not evidence of a spacetime wormhole.

Resonance / tuning fork / Phenomenon Hunter / Dao: a shared language for searching for recurring structures, while acknowledging that descriptions are not the objects they describe. A recurring structure is a hypothesis of correspondence, not an established common mechanism. Ultimodel' and the Lens are experimental instruments, not a validated theory of everything.

## Full-circle method

1. Begin with a concrete image or analogy and state what feature matters.
2. Specify a domain, inputs, outputs, transformations and an invariant.
3. Ask whether the inverse exists; if not, identify exactly what data must be retained.
4. Deliberately inject a counterexample or perturbation.
5. Compare an independent formulation or implementation, record both positive and negative results.
6. Name the missing proof obligation and refuse to convert a finite simulation into a universal conclusion.
