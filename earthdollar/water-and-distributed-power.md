# EarthDollar — Water and Distributed Power

**27 November 2026 | Guiding principle**

> Control the water and you control everything.

Water is upstream of nearly everything: agriculture, sanitation, industry, energy production and human survival.

Inspired by the water-scarcity allegory in *Rango* (2011), the EarthDollar project adopts an important philosophical distinction:

**Controlling scarcity creates power. Reducing scarcity distributes power.**

[Bone Ocean](bone-ocean-solar-steam-desalination.md) should be designed around the latter: making freshwater abundant, accessible and locally producible rather than creating another means of monopolizing it.

## Design implications

- Prioritize safe, affordable and equitable access to drinking water.
- Favor distributed production and transparent, auditable resource accounting where practical.
- Avoid monetary or ownership arrangements that make essential water access contingent on artificial scarcity.
- Measure success by reliable water delivery, affordability, environmental sustainability and resilience, not just revenue or resource ownership.

These are proposed guiding principles, not claims that desalination alone eliminates scarcity or that technology alone guarantees equitable distribution. Governance, financing, maintenance and environmental limits remain essential.

**Water as a foundation of wealth, not an instrument of control.**
