# The All Might Principle

**Everyone can.**

27 November 2026

Knowledge should be shared. Tools should be accessible. Progress should expand what ordinary people are capable of doing.

Not one person holding all the power, but everyone having the opportunity to exercise their own.

**All for one. One for all.**

## Application to EarthDollar and Bone Ocean

Reducing scarcity should distribute power rather than concentrate it. Design for accessible tools, shared knowledge, independent verification and equitable access to essential resources.

Everyone can is an aspiration for opportunity and participation, not a claim that everyone has identical abilities, resources or circumstances. Removing barriers is part of the work.
